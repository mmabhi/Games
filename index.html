How to Play "Decode It"

1
Join or Host

The host creates a room and shares the link or QR code. Players join from their smartphones or computers.

2
Decode the Emojis

Each round displays emoji clues representing a Movie or Song (e.g. 🦁👑 = The Lion King).

3
Use the Letter Blanks

Word and letter patterns (e.g. _ _ _ _ _) show you how many words and characters are in the answer!

4
Speed Matters!

The 1st player to guess gets 100 points, 2nd gets 80 points, 3rd gets 60 points, plus speed bonuses for remaining time!
